Theatre for Youth

Jump into a fun filled week of acting, improv, song, dance and play led by Annie Calhoun! Ms. Calhoun specializes in creating community, growth and confidence in young people through theatre. She’s taught theatre for over fifteen years from elementary grades through college and beyond. She is currently a theatre professor at Lewis University in Chicago.
