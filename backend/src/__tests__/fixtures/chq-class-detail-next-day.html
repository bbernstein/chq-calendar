If Chocolate Brings You Joy: Wednesday Session

As Chautauqua explores food systems and gathering during Weeks 8 and 9, chocolatier Jill Sandler will discuss the fragility of cacao, the creation of chocolate, and how one can incorporate whimsy and fun into chocolate flavors, structures, and gatherings. Students will enjoy substantial samples of Jill’s gourmet Belgian chocolate-based treats. This single day course is being offered on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.
